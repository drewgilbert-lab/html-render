## What Does Share of Voice Measure?

Share of Voice is the percentage of all brand mentions in AI answers to a tracked
prompt set that name a given brand. It is a relative metric: it moves when a
competitor's mentions move, even if your own count is unchanged.
:::

:::section
id: formula
band: tinted

## How Is Share of Voice Calculated?

```formula
text: Share of Voice = (your brand mentions / all brand mentions) x 100
```

Count every brand named across the answer set, then divide.
