      a: Four metrics anchor AI visibility measurement: **Share of Voice** (a brand's proportion of total mentions against named competitors), **Share of Model** (how one specific AI model represents a brand), **Mention Rate** (how often a brand appears at all across a tracked prompt set), and **Citation Rate** (how often a brand's own content is credited as a source).
    - q: Is there a standard definition of AI Share of Voice?
      a: Not as of Q3 2026. Vendors, agencies, and in-house teams define Share of Voice and Mention Rate differently depending on which AI engines they track and how many prompts they sample, which is why any GEO program should publish its own methodology note alongside the number.
    - q: What is the difference between Mention Rate and Citation Rate?
      a: Mention Rate counts answers where a brand name appears, with or without a link. Citation Rate is stricter and counts only answers where the brand's own content was retrieved and credited as a source. Citation Rate is the metric tied to a specific, attributable URL.
    - q: Which AI visibility metric should a team track first?
      a: Mention Rate, in almost every case. It needs only a defined prompt set and no competitor benchmark, so a team can establish a baseline in one measurement cycle and add competitive metrics once the prompt set has stabilized.
    - q: How many prompts does a tracked prompt set need?
      a: Ten to twenty real buyer questions is enough to establish a first baseline. TrustRadius's GEO Monitoring Dashboard tracks up to 100 prompts per product per month, which is a practical ceiling for a mature program rather than a starting requirement.
    - q: How often do these metrics change?
      a: Month over month, even with no new content published. AI answer engines regenerate responses continuously as recrawling updates each engine's underlying source set, so the same tracked prompt can return a different mix of brand mentions from one cycle to the next.

## What Is Core AI Visibility Metrics and Vocabulary, and Where Does It Fit in Measuring AI Search Visibility?

```section
eyebrow: Scope & Strategic Fit
id: scope
nav_label: What This Cluster Covers
```

Core AI Visibility Metrics and Vocabulary is the vocabulary layer beneath [How to Measure Your Brand's AI Search Visibility](https://hginsights.com/geo/how-to-measure-ai-search-visibility/), the parent pillar that covers the full discipline of tracking how a brand performs inside generative answer engines. Where the pillar addresses program-level strategy, ownership, and cadence, this cluster addresses definitions and arithmetic.

Four metrics anchor the vocabulary. Share of Voice measures a brand's proportion of total mentions across all AI answer sources for a defined prompt set, benchmarked against named competitors. Share of Model, a newer term, measures how a specific AI model represents a brand relative to competitors, independent of mention volume. Mention Rate measures bare appearance frequency, and Citation Rate measures credited sourcing.[^1]

None of the four terms has a single industry-standard definition yet. Vendors, agencies, and platforms define Share of Voice and Mention Rate differently depending on which AI engines they track and how many prompts they sample, which is why this cluster opens with a page on why no measurement standard exists before defining any individual term.[^3]

## How Do You Calculate Each of the Four Metrics?

```section
eyebrow: Calculation Methodology
id: calculation
nav_label: Calculation Methodology
```

Every metric in this cluster reduces to a count over a fixed denominator, which is why calculation discipline matters more than formula complexity. Share of Voice divides a brand's mentions by the combined mentions of that brand and every named competitor. Mention Rate and Citation Rate divide appearances by the number of tracked prompts in the set.

The difficulty is never the arithmetic. It is holding the prompt set, the competitor set, and the engine list constant between cycles so two numbers are actually comparable. Both calculation guides in this cluster treat that constancy as the first requirement and the formula as the second.

1. **Fix the prompt set first.** A prompt list that changes between cycles makes every trend line meaningless, regardless of how the metric is computed.
2. **Name the competitor set explicitly.** Share of Voice and Share of Model are undefined without a fixed list of competitor brands to count against.
3. **Record the engine list with every number.** A Share of Voice figure from three engines is not comparable to one from five, so the engine list belongs in the metric definition, not in a footnote.

## Which AI Visibility Metric Should a Team Track First?

```section
eyebrow: Metric Selection
id: selection
nav_label: Choosing a Metric
```

Metric selection depends on program maturity rather than preference. A team with no prior AI visibility data should start with Mention Rate, because it requires only a defined prompt set and produces a usable baseline in a single cycle. Competitive metrics come second, once the prompt set has stopped changing.

Teams reporting to a CMO or board usually need Share of Voice, because leadership questions are comparative: are named competitors ahead of us. Teams proving the return on a specific content investment need Citation Rate, because it is the only metric tied to a particular URL.

## How Should GEO Teams Evaluate Which AI Visibility Metric to Track?

```section

id: matrix
nav_label: Evaluation Matrix
subtitle: This matrix routes a use case to its best-fit metric; it is a self-selection guide for readers, not a vendor comparison.
```

| Use Case | Best-Fit Metric | What It Shows | Minimum Data Needed | Typical Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board-level AI visibility narrative | Share of Voice | Total mention share versus named competitors across all AI answer sources | Full competitor set plus a tracked prompt list | Monthly or quarterly |
| Model-specific content or PR tuning | Share of Model | How one AI model represents the brand independent of mention volume | Per-model prompt tracking on one or more engines | Monthly |
| Early-stage GEO baseline, no prior data | Mention Rate | Whether the brand appears at all in tracked AI responses | A defined prompt set; no competitor benchmark required | Monthly |
| Proving specific content or page ROI | Citation Rate | Percentage of tracked prompts where a named source is credited | Prompt-level citation tracking tied to source URLs | Monthly |
| Competitive displacement tracking | Share of Voice + Share of Model | Combined view of mention share and model-level representation | Full competitor set across two or more AI engines | Quarterly |
| Renewal or QBR proof point | Citation Rate | Coverage rate trend across the contract period | Historical citation data across the tracked prompt set | Quarterly |
